Bambu Lab H2D

FDM 3D printing · Two identical printers in the makerspace

These printers build three-dimensional objects by depositing thin layers of melted plastic filament, one layer at a time.

What can you make?

Prototypes, organizers, custom parts, models, and other three-dimensional projects.

What makes the H2D different?

The H2D uses a dual-nozzle system, which supports prints involving two materials or colors, depending on the setup.

Maximum single-nozzle build volume: 325 × 320 × 325 mm. Actual usable space depends on the print mode.

Both printers are the same model, so one information marker covers the pair.
